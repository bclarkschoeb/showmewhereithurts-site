About the host

Meet The Host

Blair has seen healthcare from every angle. For nearly 30 years she's helped build healthcare companies and bring new treatments to patients.

She has also been the patient and the caregiver in the waiting room, and has provided patient care as a first responder.

Drawing on that perspective, Blair has candid conversations with guests who share their firsthand experiences and ideas for improving healthcare.
